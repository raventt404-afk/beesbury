Our Story

A café born from obsession.

Grind & Bloom started as a farmers' market stall in 2019 — a single-origin pour-over stand run by two friends who believed good coffee deserved the same attention as fine wine.

In 2021 we opened our first brick-and-mortar space. Today we roast our own beans, bake fresh every morning, and serve a community of regulars who've become family.

We care about the whole chain: from the farmer who grows the bean, to the barista who pulls your shot, to the moment you close your eyes and take the first sip.

🌱

Ethical Sourcing

Direct trade with 6 farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.
