Our Story

Born from flame,
perfected by time.

Ember & Oak was founded on a singular belief: that great food needs nothing more than exceptional ingredients and honest fire. We source dry-aged beef from regenerative farms, pair it with wood harvested from our own oak grove, and let the flame do the rest.

Our kitchen is led by Chef Marco Velletti, who trained under three Michelin-starred chefs across Florence, Tokyo, and Buenos Aires before bringing that knowledge home to a single, intimate dining room of 48 seats.

Every service is a ritual. We don't rush. We don't compromise.
